⚠️ Our promise: honesty first

Crypto recovery is hard, and many “recovery services” are scams themselves. We hold ourselves to a clear standard:

  • We never guarantee recovery or a success rate.
  • We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or wallet password.
  • We will never contact you first or ask you to “unlock” funds by paying a release fee.
  • We encourage you to report every theft to the FBI at ic3.gov and to local police.

Frequently asked questions


Can you guarantee I’ll get my crypto back?

No. Nobody honestly can. Outcomes depend on how the funds moved and whether they reached a regulated exchange. We will tell you plainly if a case looks unlikely.

What do you need from me?

Transaction IDs, wallet addresses, dates, screenshots and any communication with the scammer. Never your seed phrase or private keys.

Do you serve clients outside Florida?

Yes. We are headquartered in Miami, Florida and work with clients remotely across the U.S.

How are fees structured?

The case review and feasibility assessment are free. Recovery fees are only charged if funds are recovered, and full terms are provided in writing before any work begins.
